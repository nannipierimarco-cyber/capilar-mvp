Rebuild the Nilo homepage in a simpler, clearer structure.

Important:
- Keep the current working app stable.
- Modify only src/app/page.tsx and small homepage-only components if needed.
- Do not touch quiz, Supabase, AI report, admin, doctor portal, membership, checkout, Flow or payments.
- Do not copy MEDVi layout exactly.
- Use Nilo branding.
- Background should be white.
- Keep design simple, premium and mobile-first.
- Do not over-explain AI.

Goal:
Simplify the homepage to 4 sections only:

1. Hero
2. Qué estás comprando
3. Cómo funciona
4. CTA final

Remove or hide redundant standalone sections:
- “Seguimiento capilar con inteligencia”
- duplicated “Cómo funciona”
- duplicated “Qué incluye”
- any repeated explanation of AI/check-ins/history.
