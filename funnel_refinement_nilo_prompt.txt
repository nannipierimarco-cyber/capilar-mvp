Refine the Nilo capillary funnel based on recent UX learnings from a clean medical D2C funnel, but do NOT copy any external brand, text, illustrations, image style, layout, or exact flow.

Important:
- Use Nilo branding, not BOE.
- Do not copy external images, drawings, text, UI, or branding.
- Keep the product compliant: no diagnosis, no guaranteed prescription, no guaranteed medication, no guaranteed results.
- The doctor makes the final medical decision.
- The platform can use AI as a support layer for organization, follow-up and history, but AI does not diagnose or prescribe.
- Medication is only prescribed if clinically appropriate and dispensed/prepared by an authorized pharmacy.
- Do not break current Supabase saving, AI report generation, doctor portal, admin, photos, or Flow payment integration.

Context:
Nilo has two core user journeys:
1. Frenar la caída
2. Evaluar trasplante / recuperación capilar

The funnel should be clean, premium, mobile-first, and easier to understand.
It should feel like a guided capillary assessment, not like a generic form.

Main feedback to incorporate:
A. We like a simpler intro, but Nilo must also capture people who are already interested in hair transplant.
B. We like the clean progress bar, back arrow and one-question-per-screen format.
C. We like one question per screen, but the funnel should not become too long.
D. We want a visual hair-loss pattern question with original images/illustrations, same logic but not copied.
E. We want to show a product recommendation around “Dutasteride + Minoxidil”, but with clear disclosure that it is subject to medical review + authorized pharmacy + delivery + AI follow-up.
F. We like a clear final summary page before payment.
G. We like a hero image showing a before/after hair contrast, but use our own generated image if available.
