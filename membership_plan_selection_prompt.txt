Update the Nilo capillary funnel by adding a membership plan selection page before checkout.

Use the existing funnel refinement context as base:
- Nilo has two journeys:
  1. Frenar la caída
  2. Evaluar trasplante / recuperación capilar
- Do not copy BOE branding, text, images, exact layout, or exact UI.
- Use Nilo branding.
- Keep compliance: no diagnosis, no guaranteed prescription, no guaranteed medication, no guaranteed results.
- The doctor makes the final medical decision.
- Medication is only prescribed if clinically appropriate and dispensed/prepared by an authorized pharmacy.
- Do not break Supabase saving, AI report generation, doctor portal, admin, photos, or Flow payment integration.

New requirement:
Add a new membership selection page after the treatment recommendation / plan page and before checkout.

Flow should become:
quiz → results/recommendation → plan page → membership selection → checkout → Flow payment

Goal:
The user should choose the duration of the membership they want to pay for:
- 6 months
- 3 months
- 1 month

Use the same pricing structure:
- 6 meses: $19.990/mes, total $119.940, badge “Más popular”, badge “Ahorra 33%”
- 3 meses: $24.990/mes, total $74.970, badge “Ahorra 17%”
- 1 mes: $29.990/mes, total $29.990

Important:
There is an additional shipping cost of $3.990.
Do not hide it.
However, shipping can be communicated in the checkout summary page instead of the membership selection page if that keeps the selection cleaner.
Recommended:
- Membership selection page shows plan prices.
- Checkout page shows:
  - selected membership subtotal
  - envío: $3.990
  - total final
