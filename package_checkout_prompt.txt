Implement the final conversion flow for the capillary MVP: package selection + payment/reservation page.

Context:
The current app has landing, quiz, results, plans, checkout and success.
We now need to add a stronger final funnel step inspired by MEDVi:
- The user sees the recommended program/package.
- The package clearly explains what is included.
- The user chooses a package/monthly option.
- Then the user goes to payment/reservation.
- The payment page must communicate that the charge/reservation is subject to medical review.

Important positioning:
We are not a clinic.
We are not a pharmacy.
We do not sell medications directly as merchant principal.
We sell a complete capillary program:
- medical review;
- prescription if clinically appropriate;
- coordination with authorized pharmacy;
- monthly treatment coordination;
- home delivery;
- WhatsApp/AI-assisted follow-up;
- photo tracking and consolidated hair history;
- clinic referral if procedure evaluation makes sense.

Important compliance:
- Do not say “approved”.
- Do not say “prescription guaranteed”.
- Do not guarantee results.
- Do not say “AI diagnoses alopecia”.
- Do not say “we sell minoxidil/dutasteride directly”.
- Say that medication is prescribed only if clinically appropriate and dispensed/prepared by an authorized pharmacy.
- It is okay to mention that the doctor may evaluate options such as oral minoxidil magistral and dutasteride/finasteride, but do not present fixed doses or a guaranteed medication bundle.

Goal:
Create a MEDVi-inspired package and payment flow, premium, mobile-first, trustworthy.

Flow should become:
results → plans/package selection → checkout/payment reservation → success

8. Keep route-specific logic

If the user journey is treatment / frenar caída:
The package page should emphasize:
- medical review;
- magistral oral minoxidil as a possible option evaluated by physician;
- dutasteride/finasteride as possible physician-evaluated options;
- pharmacy coordination;
- home delivery;
- WhatsApp/AI follow-up;
- photo history.

If the user journey is transplant / recuperación:
The package page should emphasize:
- medical review;
- photos and background review;
- whether stabilization may be needed before transplant;
- checklist before clinic evaluation;
- possible pre-op exams defined by clinic/doctor;
- clinic partner referral if appropriate;
- consolidated history before reaching clinic.

Do not over-emphasize PRP as a first-level product. Use “procedimientos clínicos complementarios” instead.
