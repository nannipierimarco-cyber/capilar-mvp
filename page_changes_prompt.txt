Create two route-specific value proposition sections before checkout.

Context:
The user reaches this after completing the quiz and seeing their result. We need to show clearly what they are buying before checkout.

There are two journeys:
1. Frenar caída
2. Evaluar trasplante / recuperación capilar

Important:
- The platform is not a clinic and not a pharmacy.
- The platform sells a complete capillary solution: medical review, coordination, treatment if clinically appropriate, pharmacy coordination, delivery, follow-up, history and clinic referral if needed.
- Do not prescribe or guarantee results.
- Do not say “approved”.
- Do not show exact medication doses.
- Do not imply we sell medications directly.
