Implement Sprint 2: simplify the quiz and make it more MEDVi-inspired.

Context:
The current quiz has 3 journeys: treatment, transplant, both.
We want to simplify this to 2 journeys:
1. Frenar caída
2. Evaluar trasplante

Important:
- Remove the third journey option.
- Do not mention PRP as a primary choice. Users may not know what PRP is.
- PRP can appear later as “tratamientos complementarios en clínica” where relevant.
- Do not show pricing on the homepage.
- Pricing should only appear after the assessment/results stage.
- Keep personal data at the end of the quiz.
- Keep compliance-safe language.

Detailed changes:

1. Landing page:
- Remove visible pricing from the homepage.
- The home should sell the complete solution, not plans/prices.
- CTA should remain “Comenzar evaluación”.
- Keep the medication/farmacy authorized compliance note near the hero.
- Do not show Plan Inicio or Plan Continuidad prices on home.

2. Quiz journey selection:
Replace the 3 options with 2 options:

Option A:
Title: “Frenar caída”
Subtitle: “Quiero mantener mi pelo, reducir la caída y empezar tratamiento médico si corresponde.”

Option B:
Title: “Evaluar trasplante”
Subtitle: “Quiero saber si soy candidato a un trasplante capilar y conocer mis opciones.”

3. Quiz flow:
- After journey selection, go directly into diagnostic questions.
- Keep personal data collection at the end.
- Keep photos near the end.
- Add short positive transition screens similar to MEDVi after key steps, using compliant copy:
  “Perfecto. Con esta información podemos orientar mejor tu evaluación.”
  “Ya casi terminamos. Ahora necesitamos revisar algunos antecedentes importantes.”

4. Social proof / trust interstitial:
- Add a screen after the first diagnostic section.
- It should feel similar to MEDVi’s testimonial section but without fake testimonials.
- Use title:
  “No estás solo: la mayoría posterga el tratamiento por no saber por dónde empezar.”
- Add 3 trust cards:
  a. “Evaluación guiada”
  b. “Médico revisa tu caso”
  c. “Farmacia autorizada y seguimiento”
- If there are no real testimonials, do not invent patient quotes.
- Add placeholder area where future video testimonials can be inserted.

5. Route-specific logic:
If user selects “Frenar caída”:
- Ask treatment-related questions:
  - Have you used minoxidil?
  - Have you used finasteride or dutasteride?
  - Did you have side effects?
  - Are you also open to a clinical evaluation if needed?
- Later result should recommend Plan Inicio if appropriate.

If user selects “Evaluar trasplante”:
- Ask transplant-related questions:
  - Have you had a transplant before?
  - How soon would you like to do it?
  - Approximate budget
  - Are you interested in financing?
  - Have you used medical treatment before to stabilize hair loss?
- Later result should recommend clinical evaluation, and mention treatment may be needed to stabilize native hair if clinically appropriate.

6. Results:
- Results should reflect only two routes.
- For “Frenar caída”:
  CTA: “Empezar Plan Inicio — $49.990”
- For “Evaluar trasplante”:
  CTA: “Solicitar evaluación de trasplante”
  Secondary note: “Si corresponde, también podemos orientarte sobre tratamiento médico para estabilizar la caída.”

7. Plans page:
- Keep pricing on plans page.
- Do not show pricing on homepage.
- If user completed intake, CTA goes to checkout.
- If no intake, CTA goes to quiz.

8. Compliance:
- Do not say prescription approved.
- Do not guarantee results.
- Do not say AI diagnoses alopecia.
- Say medication is prescribed only if clinically appropriate and dispensed by an authorized pharmacy.
- Do not invent testimonials or patient counts.
